MediGallery — Clinical Media, Patient Records & Multi-Clinic Management

MediGallery is a clinical photography and case management system engineered for dentists, dermatologists, and healthcare practitioners. Securely organize patient charts, treatment timelines, procedural notes, and multi-branch clinic workflows.

Get MediGallery on Google Play

Application Purpose & Overview

MediGallery is built specifically to address the unique documentation and workflow requirements of healthcare practices. Rather than mixing clinical photographs into personal device galleries, MediGallery provides a dedicated, encrypted environment where practitioners can:

  • Maintain Patient-Specific Records: Securely categorize high-resolution diagnostic images, intraoral photos, and short surgical videos into individual patient charts.
  • Document Treatment Progress: Attach timestamped clinical notes, diagnosis notes, and observations directly to patient files for seamless continuity of care.
  • Manage Multiple Practice Branches: Allow doctors practicing across different dental clinics or hospital departments to switch clinic environments with completely isolated data.
  • Conduct Case Presentations: Utilize interactive before-and-after comparison tools chairside to clearly explain treatment plans to patients.

🔒 How MediGallery Uses Google Sign-In & User Data

MediGallery integrates Google Sign-In strictly to provide secure, frictionless authentication for authorized healthcare practitioners. We adhere to strict data minimization principles:

  • Basic Authentication Only: We only request access to basic Google account profile information (your Google User ID, Name, and Email address) to create your practitioner profile and authenticate your access.
  • No Access to Sensitive Google Data: MediGallery does NOT request, access, read, or store data from Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, Gmail, or any other personal Google services.
  • Data Protection & Privacy: All patient records, clinical notes, and media files are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3.
  • No Third-Party Sale or Advertising: Google user data and clinical data are never sold, rented, or used for commercial advertising or data mining.
